Enter your current public IPv4 address, without /32. Lambda restricts the template's AllowedCidr parameter to that IP. Do not use a private LAN address such as 192.168.x.x.

How do I find my public IPv4 address?

On your Windows PC, open PowerShell and run:

(Invoke-RestMethod -Uri 'https://checkip.amazonaws.com').Trim()

Copy the IPv4 address printed by the command into the field above. Do not add /32; the Lambda function adds that automatically.

Alternatively, open AWS Check IP in your browser and copy the displayed address.

Use your internet-facing public IPv4 address, not the private address shown by ipconfig. If your ISP or VPN changes your public IP, the existing /32 ingress rule will not automatically update.
